About

The Dissonance Router keeps two profiles: what you say you prefer and what you actually do. When they clash, it uses a stronger model and tells you why.

1You saySet how you like answers in the mirror: tone, length, sourcing, structure, revision.
2You doIt learns from what you ask for and what you 👍 or 👎, with a quote as evidence.
3It routesThe bigger the gap, the stronger the model: Nano, Super or Ultra, with the reason logged.
Honest by design

Failures are shown with reason codes, simulated numbers are labelled, links the agent can't verify are flagged, and the "right tier" in reports is our own annotation.
